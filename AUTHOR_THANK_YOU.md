# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Haoyue Dai, Zeyu Tang, Peter Spirtes, and Kun Zhang for
formalizing the distinction between static and evolutionary selection and its
causal-discovery consequences. This independent audit records a deliberately
bounded clean-room check so readers can distinguish finite graph evidence from
the paper's general guarantees and real-world analyses.

The authors did not review, endorse, or maintain this independent repository.
